1. With the nut flush or top flush on board, bet for value on every street — don't slow-play. Size 60-80% pot on turn and river to extract maximum.
2. With nut flush draws (Ah + heart on two-heart board), semi-bluff the flop with a bet of ~50-66% pot rather than checking passively. If you pick up additional equity on the turn (e.g., gutshot, pair), continue barreling 55-70% pot. Do NOT check twice with the nut flush draw — this is a costly missed opportunity for fold equity and pot-building.
3. In the big blind facing limps preflop, check with marginal-to-good hands (suited aces, broadways) to see a cheap flop — these hands play well postflop with implied odds.
4. Raise or re-raise preflop with premium hands (AQs+, AQo+, TT+, KQs). NEVER flat-call with QQ+ or AK — always raise or 3-bet. Standard open-raise to 2.5-3x BB; 3-bet to 3-4x the open raise.
5. When you flop top pair or better (especially two pair+), bet for value on every street. Don't slow-play strong made hands — size 50-75% pot on flop, 60-80% pot on turn and river.
6. With pocket pairs TT-QQ on A-high or K-high flops, exercise pot control. Check-call at most one street — do NOT build large pots with an unimproved underpair. Fold to heavy aggression.
7. With strong draws (flush draws, open-enders, combo draws), default to betting/raising rather than checking/calling. Aggression with draws builds pots for when you hit and wins pots immediately when opponents fold.
8. Bluff when you see fit, also try to figure out if other people are bluffing (update this rule as you learn more).
