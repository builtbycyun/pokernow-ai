1. On draw-heavy flops (two-tone, connected boards), bet strong made hands (top pair top kicker or better) for value and to charge draws. Do not slow-play on wet textures.
2. With premium hands preflop (pocket pairs 99+, suited aces, AKo, AQo, KQo, KQs), ALWAYS RAISE or 3-bet. NEVER flat-call. These must be raised to build the pot and narrow the field. Flat-calling with premiums is a critical leak.
3. NEVER fold AA or KK preflop under any circumstances. NEVER fold QQ or JJ preflop to a single raise. With AA/KK, always re-raise (3-bet or 4-bet) when facing a raise. Getting all-in preflop with AA or KK is always acceptable.
4. Do not flat-call preflop with strong pocket pairs (TT+) and then fold to further aggression without seeing a flop. Either raise/re-raise for value or commit to seeing a flop if you enter the pot.
5. Fold low suited connectors and weak suited hands (like 35s, 24s, 46s) preflop from early/middle position. From late position, open-raising as a steal is acceptable, but do NOT call raises with these hands. If the steal gets re-raised, fold.
6. Fold ALL low and middling offsuit connectors and one-gappers (98o and below: 78o, 67o, 56o, 87o, 86o, 97o, etc.) preflop in multiway pots. These hands play poorly without the flush draw backup.
7. Fold weak king-x offsuit hands (K8o and below) preflop. Only play K9o+ offsuit from late position; prefer suited king-x hands.
8. Fold weak ace-x offsuit hands (A7o and below) preflop, especially facing a raise. Only play A8o+ offsuit from late position; prefer suited ace-x.
9. Fold weak queen-x offsuit hands (Q9o and below) preflop. Only play QTo+ offsuit from late position. For suited queen-x, fold Q7s and below. Only play Q8s+ from late position, QTs+ from any position.
10. Fold weak jack-x and ten-x offsuit hands (J9o and below, T8o and below) preflop in multiway pots. Only play JTo+ offsuit from late position.
11. Do not call preflop with mediocre offsuit hands (J9o, T9o, Q8o, etc.) just because they look connected.
12. In SB vs BB (heads-up blind) situations, widen your opening range significantly. Raise with most hands that have any playability (any suited hand, any ace, any king, connected cards, hands with a ten or higher). Do not fold playable hands like T7o+ or suited hands in this spot — you're heads-up with position disadvantage but good pot odds.
13. Bluff when you see fit, also try to figure out if other people are bluffing (update this rule as you learn more).
