ABSOLUTE FOLD: Always fold hands with no connectivity, no suited value, and no high cards (e.g., 72o, 73o, 82o, 83o, 42o, 43o, 52o, 53o) regardless of position or action. These are unplayable.
Fold weak/marginal hands facing preflop raises: this includes low connectors (6-5o, 7-6o), low suited cards, and weak broadway hands like Q8o, K5o, J7o. These hands are frequently dominated and play poorly postflop.
Only enter raised pots (as a caller) with hands that have strong high-card value (KQ+, AJ+), pocket pairs, or suited connectors (suited only) in position with good pot odds.
Fold speculative hands (low connectors, low suited cards, weak queen/king hands) facing a raise or 3-bet preflop unless in late position with favorable pot odds and multiway action expected.
Button open-raise range in 3-handed play can be wide: any pocket pair, any suited ace, suited connectors 54s+, suited one-gappers 75s+, any broadway, and offsuit connectors 89o+. Fold true junk (offsuit disconnected low cards).
Fold weak ace-rag hands (A4o, A5o, A6o, A3o etc.) preflop when facing a raise. These hands have poor playability postflop.
From the big blind facing a 3x raise, fold offsuit ace-rag (A2o-A9o). The pot odds do not justify calling with hands that flop weak pairs and face difficult postflop decisions.
Defend the big blind with suited aces A7s-A9s facing a single raise (up to 3x). These hands have enough equity from flush draws and decent pair potential to justify a call. Fold suited aces A2s-A6s unless multiway and cheap.
Only play low suited aces (A2s-A6s) from late position when the price is cheap and multiway action is expected.
BB defense range facing a raise should be limited to: pocket pairs, suited broadways, suited connectors 54s+, suited aces A7s+, and offsuit broadways KJo+/QJo. Fold everything else.
Distinguish between open-raising ranges (can be wide from late position) and calling ranges facing a raise (should be tighter). Low suited connectors like 85s and 75s are fine opens from the button but should be folded facing a raise.
Bluff when you see fit, also try to figure out if other people are bluffing (update this rule/idea as you learn more).
